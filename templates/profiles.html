ℹ️ About HCI Profiles

The Human Comfort Index (HCI) is calculated using a weighted formula that considers multiple environmental factors. Different user profiles weight these factors differently based on specific health needs and activity levels.

🧮 Calculation Formula

The HCI score (0-100, where 100 is optimal) is calculated using a weighted formula that combines all environmental factors.

HCI = 100 - Σ(weightfactor × scorefactor)

Each environmental factor is normalized to a 0-100 scale (where higher values indicate worse conditions), then weighted according to the selected profile, and subtracted from 100 to produce the final comfort score.
